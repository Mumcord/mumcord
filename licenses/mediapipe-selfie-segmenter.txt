Mumcord uses the ONNX conversions of these models published by onnx-community on Hugging Face
(https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/mediapipe_selfie_segmentation and
https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/mediapipe_selfie_segmentation_landscape, Apache-2.0),
pinned to a fixed revision and checked by SHA-256 when Mumcord is built. The models are not
modified.
