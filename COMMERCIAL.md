## AGPL in a nutshell

- You can use, study, change and share Mumcord for free.
- If you distribute Mumcord, a modified version, or a product built on it, you must publish the complete source code under the AGPL as well.
- The AGPL also applies if users interact with a modified version over a network.

This is a plain summary, not legal advice. The license text in [LICENSE](LICENSE) is what counts.
